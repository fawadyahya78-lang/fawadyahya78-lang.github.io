--- meta-viewport: width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0 title: Privacy Policy - Fusion Reflex --- # Privacy Policy for Fusion Reflex **Effective Date:** May 31, 2026 **Last Updated:** May 31, 2026 Fawad Yahya ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Fusion Reflex mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App. ## 1. Information We Collect **Information You Provide:** - **Player Name:** A nickname you choose for the leaderboard. This is publicly visible to other players on the leaderboard. - **Country:** A country you select to represent on the leaderboard. This is publicly visible. - **Game Scores:** Your high scores are stored to compete on global, reaction-time, and country-based leaderboards. **Automatically Collected Information:** - **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version, and app version (anonymous). - **Advertising Identifiers:** If you allow tracking, advertising identifiers may be used by ad networks to show personalized ads. ## 2. How We Use Information - To enable global, reaction-time, and country-based leaderboards - To display advertisements (via Google AdMob) - To process in-app purchases (via Apple App Store) - To improve game experience ## 3. Third-Party Services The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information: - **Google Firebase Firestore:** Stores leaderboard data. [Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) - **Google AdMob:** Displays advertisements. [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) - **Apple App Store:** Processes in-app purchases. [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) ## 4. Children's Privacy Our App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will delete it. ## 5. Data Storage and Security Leaderboard data is stored on Google Firebase servers in Europe (europe-west region). Local data (high scores, settings, extra lives) is stored only on your device. ## 6. Your Rights You may: - Choose any nickname (no real name required) - Stop using the App at any time - Request deletion of your leaderboard entry by contacting us - Disable advertising tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy > Tracking ## 7. In-App Purchases The App offers the following in-app purchases: - **Remove Ads** (one-time purchase) - **10 Lives Pack** (consumable) - **25 Lives Pack** (consumable) Payment is processed by Apple. We do not store your payment information. ## 8. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. ## 9.
